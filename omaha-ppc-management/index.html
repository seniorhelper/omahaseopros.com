Why Run Both

Ads are a faucet. Search is a well.

Turn ads off and the traffic stops that afternoon. Turn SEO off and it keeps producing for months. Neither fact makes one better — it makes them different tools, and using one when you need the other is how marketing budgets get wasted.

Weeks

How long paid search takes to tell you whether an offer converts. That speed is the real product. You are not just buying clicks, you are buying an answer.

A paid campaign is the fastest honest test of a message you will ever run. Two weeks and a modest budget will tell you which offer people respond to, which words make them click, and which landing page loses them. That knowledge then goes into everything else — your homepage, your local SEO, your signage, what your team says on the phone.

Most agencies keep those two disciplines in separate rooms. We do not, because the thing paid search learns is exactly the thing organic content needs to know.

Months

How long competitive organic rankings take. Slower, but you stop paying per click, and the position does not vanish when the card declines.

The businesses that get this right run ads while the search work matures, then throttle ad spend back as organic picks up the same searches. You are not paying twice for the same customer — you are paying for coverage while the cheaper channel gets built.

And when an AI answer panel sits above the organic results, paid placement still appears. That matters more in 2026 than it did two years ago. More on that on our AI search page.

What We Run

Four campaign types, and when each one earns its place.

Search campaigns

Text ads against what people type. The highest-intent traffic on the internet, because somebody is describing their problem in their own words at the moment they have it. This is where most local budgets should start.

Display campaigns

Visual ads across the web. Not for chasing direct response — for launches, awareness, and staying visible in a market where nobody knows your name yet. We are upfront about which job it is doing.

Remarketing

Reaching people who already visited. Warmer than any cold audience because they raised their hand once. Capped on frequency, because following somebody for ninety days breeds resentment, not sales.

Local and call-focused campaigns

Built for a phone to ring rather than a form to fill. Call extensions, location targeting, hours-aware scheduling. For a lot of Omaha service businesses this is the only campaign that matters.

How We Run It

The unglamorous things that decide whether ads work.

Campaign structure is not the hard part. These are.

  • Tracking before spending. Conversion tracking and the pixel go in first. Running ads without them is buying traffic you cannot evaluate.
  • Negative keywords, constantly. Half of paid search management is stopping you paying for searches that were never going to buy. This is where most wasted budget hides.
  • The landing page is the campaign. A great ad pointing at a slow, vague page loses. We will fix the page before we raise the bid.
  • Phone calls counted as conversions. For service businesses most of the value arrives by phone. If calls are not tracked, the reporting is fiction.
  • Search terms reviewed, not just keywords. The search terms report shows what people actually typed. It is the most useful screen in the account and the most ignored.
  • Sensible change cadence. Campaigns need volume before numbers mean anything. Optimizing after three days is reacting to noise.
  • Your account stays yours. We manage inside an account you own. You keep the history if we part ways.
  • Honest channel advice. If your money would do more in local SEO or local community reach this quarter, we say so.
Straight Answers

Questions we get.

How much should an Omaha business spend on Google Ads?

Enough that the data means something. Below a certain point you are paying for a sample too small to learn from, which is worse than not running ads at all. The right floor depends on what a click costs in your industry, and that varies enormously between a plumber and a law firm. We look at your actual market before suggesting a number, and we will tell you if paid search is the wrong channel for you right now.

Is PPC better than SEO?

They answer different questions. Paid search tells you within weeks whether an offer converts and puts you at the top of the page today. SEO takes months but builds a channel you stop paying per click for. Most businesses should use paid to find the message that works and SEO to make that position permanent. If you only have budget for one, we will tell you honestly which fits your situation.

Do I own my Google Ads account if we stop working together?

Yes. We build campaigns inside an account you own, with you as the owner and us as a manager. If we part ways you keep the account, the history and the learnings. Some agencies run your ads inside their own account so you leave with nothing. Ask any agency this question before you sign anything.

What is remarketing and does it actually work?

Remarketing shows ads to people who already visited your site. It works because those people already raised their hand, which makes them far warmer than a cold audience. It is also easy to overdo. Following someone around the internet for ninety days does not make them buy, it makes them resent you. We cap frequency and set sensible windows.

Can you run display ads for a local business?

Yes, though display works differently from search. Search catches people already looking for you. Display puts you in front of people who are not looking yet, which means it is better for awareness, launches and remarketing than for direct response. We will be clear about which job it is doing before we spend your money on it.

How fast will I see results from paid ads?

Impressions and clicks start immediately. Meaningful data takes two to four weeks, because campaigns need volume before the numbers mean anything. Anybody optimizing a campaign after three days is making decisions from noise. We let it gather data, then we act on it.
