First, What This Actually Is

Owned media, not bought media.

When you buy Facebook ads, you rent thirty seconds of someone's scroll and the platform decides who sees it. A local group is different. People joined it on purpose, they check it on purpose, and the group itself has a voice they already trust.

We're the administrators of several of those groups. So when we put your business in front of that audience, we're not bidding against anyone — we're making a decision.

Three Things Nobody Else Can Offer You

What administrator access actually buys.

These aren't features you can purchase from Facebook. They come from running the group.

01

Your brand rides along on other people's posts.

When a member creates a garage sale post or an event inside the group, the group's cover image is what gets attached by default. A lot of people never change it. That means members end up publishing your creative as part of their own post — to their own friends, in their own feed.

We can't promise a specific number of those. But it's a channel that does not exist anywhere else in local marketing, and it costs nothing extra once your image is up.

02

Top of the feed, and it stays there.

Featured and pinned placement holds your post at the top of the group while everything else scrolls underneath. In a busy local group that's the difference between being seen once and being seen every single time somebody opens it.

There's no auction for this position and no algorithm deciding whether you deserve it. It's a slot, and we decide who's in it.

03

A notification straight to local phones.

Group-wide announcements push an actual phone notification to members. Not an impression — a notification, the same kind they get from a text. For a grand opening, a one-week promotion or an event, nothing else in local marketing moves that fast.

We use this sparingly and on purpose. An audience you over-message is an audience you lose, and these groups are worth more to us intact than they are burned out. If we tell you now isn't the moment for it, that's why.

See If Your Business Is A Fit

What we won't tell you about this.

We won't give you a guaranteed number of leads, because we can't know it and neither can anyone else. Group reach varies with the season, the offer and whether people find your post interesting.

We also won't flood the groups on your behalf. Members leave groups that feel like a billboard, and a group nobody reads is worth nothing to you or to us. We keep promotional placement at a level the community tolerates, which is exactly what makes the placement worth having.

What we will do is tell you honestly whether your business is a fit, and say so plainly if it isn't. There are plenty of businesses we'd point toward local SEO in Omaha instead, because that's where their customers actually are.

Straight Answers

Common questions.

Which groups do you administer?

We'll walk you through the specific groups, their size and their makeup on a call. We don't publish that list publicly, because the value of these groups depends on keeping them healthy rather than turning them into a target.

What does group placement cost?

It depends on the placement type, how long it runs and whether we're also producing the creative. Call 1-800-481-8638 and we'll quote your situation directly. We don't publish a rate card because the right number for a one-truck operation and a multi-location business are not the same number.

Do I need a website for this to work?

Not strictly. A phone number and a clear offer can work on their own. But a group post sends people somewhere, and if that somewhere is slow or confusing you lose most of them. We'd rather fix the destination first than send good traffic to a bad page.

Can I do this and SEO at the same time?

That's the combination we recommend. Group placement produces attention now while search work builds a channel that keeps producing without being paid for each time. One is a faucet, the other is a well.

Will people know it's an advertisement?

Promotional placement is presented as what it is. We don't disguise business posts as personal recommendations, because that is how you lose a community's trust permanently and it is not worth anyone's marketing budget.
