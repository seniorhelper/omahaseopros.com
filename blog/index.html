What You Can Expect From Anything Published Here.

Four rules, and they are the reason some of these articles are less flattering to us than a marketing blog usually is. Every number is attributed. No client results appear anywhere. Every tool is free and requires nothing. And when something is genuinely unknown, the article says so rather than guessing confidently.

✓

Every statistic names its source and date

If you see a number here, you will see who published it and when. "Studies show" is not a source. Where surveys disagree — and on pricing they disagree by more than three times — we show you all of them rather than picking the flattering one.

✓

No client results, ever

You will not find case study percentages, review counts or star ratings on this site. Not because we have nothing to say, but because you cannot verify any of it and a number you cannot check is decoration.

✓

Tools are free and need nothing from you

Nine of them across the site. No email address, no signup, no "unlock your results". They run in your browser and nothing is transmitted anywhere.

✓

We say when we do not know

Nobody can rank-track AI answers. Nobody can guarantee a Google position. llms.txt is unproven. These are stated plainly in the articles that touch them, because the alternative is selling certainty that does not exist.
