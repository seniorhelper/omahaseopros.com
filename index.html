    Straight Answers

    Questions Omaha business owners ask us.

    How much does SEO cost in Omaha?

    It depends on how competitive your industry is and how much needs fixing before anything can rank. We scope every project individually and quote a number only after someone has actually looked at your site. We publish six named 2026 industry pricing surveys on our pricing page so you can see the real market range before you talk to anyone.

    How long before I see results from SEO?

    Technical fixes can show up in weeks. Rankings for competitive commercial terms usually take three to six months, and sometimes longer in crowded industries. Anyone who promises faster than that is either guessing or selling you branded search you were already getting for free.

    Do you require a long-term contract?

    No. There is no long-term contract to start working with us. Custom website builds are structured as a lease with a buyout option, and we explain that in full before anything is signed. Ongoing search work is month to month.

    Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

    No, and neither can anyone else. Google's own guidance on hiring an SEO says that no one can guarantee a number one ranking. What we promise instead is that we put your business first, work on it the way we work on our own companies, and tell you the truth about what is and isn't working.

    Do you meet with clients in person?

    Yes. We meet Omaha metro clients in person when that's what they want, and we work with plenty of others entirely by phone and video. Most owners tell us a focused twenty-minute call beats a two-hour meeting, so that's our default.

    What is the difference between SEO and AI search optimization?

    SEO is about ranking in Google's list of blue links. AI search optimization, sometimes called AIO or GEO, is about being named inside the answer that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews generate. They share a foundation but they are not the same work, and in 2026 you need both.

    I already pay an SEO company. Can you tell me if they're doing anything?

    Yes, and we'll do it free. Send us your last two monthly reports and your website address. We'll tell you plainly what has changed on your site, what hasn't, and whether the numbers in those reports mean anything. If your current company is doing good work, we will tell you that too.
