    Straight Answers

    Questions Omaha business owners ask us.

    How much does SEO cost in Omaha?

    It depends on how competitive your industry is and how much needs fixing before anything can rank. We scope every project individually and quote a number only after someone has actually looked at your site. We publish six named 2026 industry pricing surveys on our pricing page so you can see the real market range before you talk to anyone.

    How long before I see results from SEO?

    Technical fixes can show up in weeks. Rankings for competitive commercial terms usually take three to six months, and sometimes longer in crowded industries. Anyone who promises faster than that is either guessing or selling you branded search you were already getting for free.

    Do you require a long-term contract?

    No. There is no long-term contract to start working with us. Custom website builds are structured as a lease with a buyout option, and we explain that in full before anything is signed. Ongoing search work is month to month.

    Can you guarantee a #1 ranking on Google?

    No, and neither can anyone else. Google's own guidance on hiring an SEO says that no one can guarantee a number one ranking. What we promise instead is that we put your business first, work on it the way we work on our own companies, and tell you the truth about what is and isn't working.

    Do you meet with clients in person?

    Yes. We meet Omaha metro clients in person when that's what they want, and we work with plenty of others entirely by phone and video. Most owners tell us a focused twenty-minute call beats a two-hour meeting, so that's our default.

    What is the difference between SEO and AI search optimization?

    SEO is about ranking in Google's list of blue links. AI search optimization, sometimes called AIO or GEO, is about being named inside the answer that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews generate. They share a foundation but they are not the same work, and in 2026 you need both.

    I already pay an SEO company. Can you tell me if they're doing anything?

    Yes, and we'll do it free. Send us your last two monthly reports and your website address. We'll tell you plainly what has changed on your site, what hasn't, and whether the numbers in those reports mean anything. If your current company is doing good work, we will tell you that too.

    What areas around Omaha do you serve?

    Omaha, Bellevue, Papillion, La Vista, Gretna, Elkhorn, Ralston, Bennington, Springfield, Waterloo, Valley, Blair and Plattsmouth, plus Council Bluffs across the river in Iowa. We also work with businesses in Lincoln and Fremont. We are a national agency and take clients in other markets too, but Omaha is one of our core hubs.

    Do I have to be in Omaha to work with you?

    No. We are a national agency and Omaha is one of our core hubs. Plenty of our clients are elsewhere. What Omaha businesses get is someone who actually knows the market rather than an agency writing about the Old Market from a thousand miles away.

    What is the very first thing you would do for my business?

    Look at it. We review your site, your competitors and where you currently rank before the first real conversation, so we are not asking you to describe your own website back to us. That review is free and you keep it whether you hire us or not.

    Do you work with small businesses or only bigger companies?

    Small businesses are most of what we do. We are not an enterprise agency and we do not pretend to be. If you are one truck, one chair or one storefront, that is a normal client for us.

    What happens if I want to stop?

    You stop. Ongoing search work is month to month and you keep everything we built, including your website code and your ad account. There is no clause that makes leaving expensive.

    Can you help if I do not have a website at all?

    Yes, and sometimes that is the easier starting point because nothing has to be undone. We build the site and the search foundation together rather than bolting SEO onto something that was never structured for it.

    How do I know the leads are coming from you?

    Call tracking and form tracking, tied to source, reported monthly. If we cannot show you which channel produced a call, we have not done our job. Rankings are a leading indicator. The phone is the scoreboard.

    What is the difference between SEO, AIO and GEO?

    SEO is ranking in Google's list of links. AIO and GEO are two names for the same newer discipline: being named inside the answer that ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews generate. They share a technical foundation but the content work is different, and in 2026 you need both.

    Do you charge for the first conversation?

    No. The first conversation, the site review and the audit of your current agency's reports are all free. We would rather you got real information and decided for yourself than felt pushed into something.
